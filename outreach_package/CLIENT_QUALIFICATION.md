# Client Qualification — Website Contact Scraper

Use this to sort incoming conversations into four buckets: good prospect, bad prospect, likely buyer, time waster.

---

## Good Prospect

A good prospect has a real need and can move quickly.

| Criteria | Signal |
|---|---|
| Clear use case | Mentions lead gen, demand gen, appointment setting, local SEO, or market research |
| Volume | Asks about 100, 500, or 1,500 websites or more |
| Data awareness | Already pays for ZoomInfo, Apollo, or manual researchers |
| Decision speed | Replies within one day and asks about next steps |
| Engagement | Asks for a sample, pricing, or how to pay |
| Respectful | Reads the email and asks relevant questions |

**Examples:**
- "We need 500 websites scraped for our SDR team. What is the cost?"
- "Can you add Instagram to the CSV? We need it for our agency."
- "This is faster than what we do manually. Send me pricing."

**Action:** Prioritize, respond fast, send sample, push for Growth/Scale tier.

---

## Bad Prospect

A bad prospect is not a fit for the offer as it exists.

| Criteria | Signal |
|---|---|
| Wrong data need | Wants LinkedIn profile scraping, email enrichment of existing lists, or B2B database access |
| Unrealistic scope | Wants millions of records or every website in a country for a tiny budget |
| Illegal ask | Requests private data, login-required data, or bypassing anti-bot protections |
| No budget | Expects free work or cannot afford even ₹2,500 |
| Wrong geography | Needs GDPR/CCPA-sensitive data without understanding compliance |
| Treated as commodity | Only compares on price and does not care about data quality |

**Examples:**
- "Can you scrape LinkedIn profiles of all CTOs in India?"
- "I need 10,000 websites for $20."
- "Can you hack into private directories?"

**Action:** Decline politely, do not send free samples, and move on.

---

## Likely Buyer

A likely buyer shows concrete buying signals within the first 1-2 replies.

| Signal | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Asks for pricing and payment method | Budget is available |
| Sends a URL list or niche immediately | They are ready to start |
| Wants to compare with current tool | Evaluating alternatives, not just browsing |
| Asks about turnaround time | Has a campaign deadline |
| Asks about source code or resell rights | Wants a long-term solution |
| Replies to follow-ups | Engaged and interested |

**Examples:**
- "How fast can you deliver 500 sites?"
- "We currently use Apollo but the data is stale. Can you beat their price?"
- "I have 230 URLs ready. Send the invoice."

**Action:** Move to FIRST_CALL_CHECKLIST or send invoice directly.

---

## Time Waster

A time waster consumes effort but will not buy.

| Signal | Why it is a waste |
|---|---|
| Asks for endless free samples | Wants free data, not a service |
| Changes requirements every reply | No clear decision |
| Wants a detailed proposal before any commitment | Likely shopping around or comparing free quotes |
| Does not answer direct questions | Not serious |
| Ghosts after receiving sample | Not a qualified buyer |
| Negotiates aggressively on price for small orders | Will churn or complain |

**Examples:**
- "Can you send me 5 different samples first?"
- "Write me a 10-page proposal."
- "Can you do 1,000 sites for ₹500?"

**Action:** Send one final polite message. If no clear next step, deprioritize and do not chase.

---

## Scoring Table

Use a 1-5 scale for each factor. Total score / 25.

| Factor | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Need clarity | Vague | Some idea | Clear niche | Specific campaign | Ready URL list |
| Volume fit | < 100 or huge | Around 100 | 250-500 | 500-1,500 | Recurring 1,500+ |
| Budget fit | No budget | ₹2,500 possible | ₹10,000 ready | ₹25,000 ready | ₹50,000 ready |
| Speed to close | Ghosting | Slow replies | Replies in 2 days | Replies same day | Asks to pay now |
| Decision power | Gatekeeper | Influencer | Manager | Director | Owner / final sign-off |

**Interpretation:**
- 22-25: Hot — send invoice or book call immediately
- 17-21: Warm — send sample and follow up aggressively
- 12-16: Lukewarm — send sample, one follow-up, then deprioritize
- Below 12: Cold / time waster — polite close, no free work

## Decision Flow

```
New reply
    │
    ▼
Is the ask illegal or impossible? ──YES──► Bad prospect → Decline
    │ NO
    ▼
Does the reply ask for price / sample / invoice? ──YES──► Likely buyer → Fast track
    │ NO
    ▼
Are they asking useful questions? ──YES──► Good prospect → Send sample
    │ NO
    ▼
Are they vague, demanding, or slow? ──YES──► Time waster → Polite close
    │ NO
    ▼
Continue nurturing with sample + follow-up
```
