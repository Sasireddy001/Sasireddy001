# First Call Checklist — Website Contact Scraper

Use this checklist on a discovery call. The goal is to qualify the prospect, size the right tier, and close the first paid engagement.

---

## Pre-Call

- [ ] Confirm the call time and send a calendar invite or message
- [ ] Open `agency_outreach_campaign.md` and review the prospect's company, score, and category
- [ ] Prepare a free 50-contact sample if they have already shared a niche or URLs
- [ ] Have `REPLY_PLAYBOOK.md`, `sales_sheet.md`, and sample CSV files open
- [ ] Decide on the target tier to recommend before the call starts

---

## Opening (2 minutes)

- [ ] Confirm the prospect's name and role
- [ ] Thank them for replying to the outreach
- [ ] Recap the offer in one sentence: "I help agencies turn website lists into CSVs with emails, phones, and social links"
- [ ] Set the agenda: "I will ask a few quick questions, show you a sample, and then we can decide the right tier"

---

## Discovery Questions

### About their business

1. What kind of clients do you primarily serve? (e.g. SaaS, real estate, dental, HVAC)
2. How do you currently build your prospect contact lists?
3. How many websites or contacts does your team research per week?
4. What is the biggest pain point in that process?

### About the data they need

5. Do you need emails, phone numbers, or both?
6. Do you use LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, or Twitter in your outreach?
7. What does your ideal CSV look like? Which columns are must-haves?
8. Do you have a list of target URLs already, or do you need help building one?

### About volume and budget

9. How many websites would you want scraped in the first order?
10. Is this a one-time need or recurring?
11. What is your budget range for this kind of data?
12. Who else is involved in the buying decision?

### About timeline

13. When do you need the first CSV delivered?
14. Do you need a sample before deciding?
15. What is your preferred turnaround for future orders?

---

## Sample & Demo (5 minutes)

- [ ] Share the screen and open the relevant sample CSV
- [ ] Explain the columns: URL, emails, phones, LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, title, description
- [ ] Point out 2-3 real extracted values
- [ ] Ask: "Does this format work for your CRM or outreach tool?"
- [ ] If they have questions about data accuracy, explain the source-linking and retry logic

---

## Pricing & Tier Recommendation

- [ ] Match the volume they mentioned to the right tier
- [ ] State the tier and price clearly
- [ ] Explain what is included and the turnaround time
- [ ] Mention the free revision for missing or malformed rows
- [ ] Ask: "Does that work for you?"

**Tier guide for the call:**

| Volume they mention | Recommend | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Under 100 sites | Starter | ₹2,500 |
| 100-500 sites | Growth | ₹10,000 |
| 500-1,500 sites | Scale | ₹25,000 |
| Want to resell / run internally | Enterprise | ₹50,000 |

---

## Closing (2 minutes)

- [ ] Confirm the tier and scope
- [ ] Ask for the target URL list or niche
- [ ] Explain payment: 100% advance via UPI or bank transfer
- [ ] Set delivery expectation: 24-48 hours after payment and URL list
- [ ] Send a summary email within 30 minutes after the call with:
  - Agreed tier and price
  - Payment details
  - URL list request
  - Delivery timeline

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

- [ ] Cannot describe what they use data for
- [ ] Unrealistic expectations (100% email coverage, real-time scraping)
- [ ] Wants free sample after free sample with no buying signal
- [ ] Refuses to share a niche, URL, or target market
- [ ] Asks for illegal or private data sources

If 2+ red flags appear, pause the sale and focus on qualification instead of closing.
