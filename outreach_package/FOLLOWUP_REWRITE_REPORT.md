## Follow-up #1

### Old version (Day 2)

```
Hi there,

Quick follow-up on the free 50-contact sample for [Company]. Happy to run it for your target ICP.

Best,
Sasidhar Mopuru
```

### New version (Day 2)

```
Hi there,

Just checking - would a sample list of [Company]'s target accounts with publicly available contact information be useful for your team?

No cost. No tech setup. I can send it as a clean CSV within 24 hours.

Best,
Sasidhar Mopuru
```

### Reason for change

- Removed "scraper," "run," and "ICP."
- Reframed the offer around a tangible outcome: a sample list of target accounts.
- Added a no-friction reassurance ("No cost. No tech setup.") to reduce the decision effort.
- Ended with a clear deliverable (clean CSV) and a 24-hour turnaround promise.

- Added a direct yes/no question to make replying effortless and reduce ambiguity.

## Recommended Follow-up Schedule

| Timing | Action | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Send initial email with free sample offer | Land in inbox |
| Day 2 | Follow-up #1 (new version) | Offer a no-friction sample list in 24 hours |
| Day 4 | Follow-up #2 (new version) | Get a clear yes/no and close the thread |
| Day 5+ | Stop active follow-up; log as "no reply" | Avoid being marked as spam |
| 60-90 days | Re-engage with a fresh sample from a different vertical | Re-open when priorities may have changed |

### A/B test plan for the 14 delivered emails

- Split the 14 active prospects into three groups:
  - **Version A:** 5 prospects (short, direct)
  - **Version B:** 5 prospects (medium, value-first)
  - **Version C:** 4 prospects (question-based)
- Track reply rate per version over 5 business days.
- Use the winning version for all future follow-ups and add it to `subject_lines_and_copy.md` as the default.
