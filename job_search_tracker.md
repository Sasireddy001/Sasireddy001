## LinkedIn job alert query

Use this for each company:

```
"Data Engineer" AND [CompanyName]
Location: Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Remote
Experience: 1-3 years
Date posted: Past 24 hours
Work mode: On-site, Hybrid, Remote
```

Click **Set alert** after each search.

## Naukri alert

- Job title: `Data Engineer`
- Keywords: `PySpark, Databricks, Kafka, Delta Lake, Microsoft Fabric`
- Location: `Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Remote`
- Experience: `0-3 years`
- Salary: `12-18 LPA`
- Notice period: `0-3 months`

## Daily routine

1. Check LinkedIn alerts (morning + evening).
2. Check company career pages for top 10 targets.
3. Apply within 3 hours if a role matches.
4. Send a referral message to one insider before or after applying.
5. Make one micro-update on Naukri to trigger the freshness algorithm.
